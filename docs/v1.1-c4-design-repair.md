# StorageInventory v1.1 C4 design repair: D-52's specification and evidence

The bounded repair that the independent re-review of decision D-52 (`docs/v1.1-c4-design-rereview.md`, verdict
`C4 DESIGN REQUIRES REVISION`) requires before the C4 implementation is repaired. It closes the re-review's one High and six
Medium findings in the authoritative sections of `docs/v1.1-preproduction-spec.md`, corrects the C4 design review's evidence
and tooling under `docs/evidence/c4-design-review/`, and publishes an erratum. It is **not** the C4 implementation repair: no
file under `src/` or `tests/` changes, the C4 review's findings C4-H01 and C4-M01 to C4-M17 stay open exactly as stated, and C5
does not start. D-52's architecture is retained unchanged.

Units: **MiB = 2^20 bytes, MB = 10^6 bytes**; decisive figures are given in bytes. Labels: **measured** (run here, raw data in
`docs/evidence/c4-design-review/repair-results/`), **from source** (read in SQLite's or StorageInventory's code), **defined** (a
value this specification sets), **inferred** (argued, not measured).

## 3. Architecture retained

Nothing below was changed or reopened:

- **T-IMPORT is one atomic rollback-journal transaction** (`BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, `TRUNCATE`, `synchronous = FULL`, verification
  inside, three final statements, one `COMMIT` as the validity point).
- **Publication stays atomic**; there is no committed intermediate state.
- **The name dictionary is per source** (`UNIQUE (source_id, utf16)`; invariant 4 per source; IMP-03, IMP-04 unchanged).
- **Hidden staged or batched commits stay rejected** for C4 (§15.4's stop condition, D-52).
- **Mandatory key-ordered interning stays rejected** (IMP-03); key-ordered lookups remain a prepared alternative.
- **The 64 MiB import cache stays the configuration** (IMP-02, IMP-08); a larger cache remains the first prepared alternative.
- **PERF-15 stays replaced** by structural journal confinement (a), free-space safety (b, IMP-11) and stop and recovery bounds (c).
- **C5 stays gated** behind an accepted C4 (§19.1: C5 can start after C1, C3 and C4), with IMP-11's constants (Q-24) as an OPEN
  item with a safe default resolved inside C5.

### 5.2 Calibration (`calibrate.py`, `census/calibration.json`)

The generator repeats vocabulary entries by rank floor(V × u^β). β is fitted to each census's share of files in its most frequent
1% of names, at the census's own distinct ratio; the length of an entry follows the census's length quantiles.

| Model | β | Top 1% share (census / model) | Top 0.1% | Top 10% | Names seen once |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| system | 13.501 | 55.06% / 55.06% | 43.4% / 46.3% | 75.0% / 67.3% | 77.3% / 53.5% |
| data | 2.558 | 7.35% / 7.35% | 3.49% / 2.81% | 25.2% / 22.5% | 53.4% / 59.7% |

The one-parameter model matches the head (which governs how many lookups the 262,144-entry name cache absorbs) and is within a few
points elsewhere, except the system drive's share of names seen once (77% against 54%): the real drive has more singletons and a
heavier head than one power law gives. The distinct ratio, which drives the dictionary's insertions and journal, is exact by
construction.

### 5.3 Objective first save (definition)

**F(n, d, m)** into a **new source** (its dictionary is empty, so the **new-name ratio equals the distinct-name ratio d**): a
vocabulary of V = round(d × n) entries, every entry occurring once and the other n − V file slots repeating entries by the model's
skew, never twice in one folder; names of the model's length distribution with a uniform spread over the key space (five leading
letters encode a bijective scramble of the rank). **Parameters:** n, d, m (system or data; it fixes the length quantiles and β); the
tree (C4's generator, about 0.25 folders per file); ω = 25% of the other source's vocabulary shared with the target's.
**Representative:** m = system, d ≤ 25% (cells: 1M and 2M at 25%). **Stress:** d ≤ 60% (cell: 2M at 60%, data model).
**Worst-case:** d > 60% (cell: 2M at 100%, every name distinct).

### 5.4 Objective re-scan (definition)

**R(n, d, m; ρ, δ, α)** into the **existing** source A, whose first snapshot p1 is F(n, d, m) and whose second, p3, is p1 re-scanned
with the routine churn: the target is p1 again with a fraction **ρ** of its files renamed to names **new to the source** (spread over
the key space), a fraction **δ deleted**, and beside a fraction **α** one file **added** whose name is already in the vocabulary.
**Parameters:** the existing dictionary (about (d + 0.01) × n names: about 520,000 at 2M, d = 25%), n, the **retained-name ratio**
1 − ρ − δ, ρ, δ, α. **Representative:** ρ ≤ 1%, δ ≤ 0.5%, α ≤ 0.5% (cells: 1M and 2M at exactly these). **Stress:** ρ ≤ 10%, δ and α
≤ 5% (cells: 2M at 5% / 2.5% / 2.5% and at 10% / 5% / 5%). **Worst-case:** ρ > 10%, or the dictionary of a source whose every name is
distinct (cell: a 1% re-scan of an N2 source).

### 5.5 Stress and worst-case boundary

Written into §15.4: a cell's class follows from its parameters alone, before it runs; a cell that fits several rows takes the
hardest; results never move a cell between classes, so a valid but slow representative run stays a representative result. The
adversarial families of the design review (N2 interleaved, N3 mixed) stay as worst-case cells; N1 (append) and 10M rows stay
informational. **Product decision (D-53):** high-novelty real sources are bounded by PERF-14's engine target and the 5 s Stop
budget, not by PERF-01; adversarial distributions are bounded by the stop thresholds only.

## 7. IMP-11: the model and the `COMMIT` write-out (C4DR-M01)

### 7.1 The rule (IMP-11 as repaired)

At every check during T-IMPORT the primitive reads, on the writer inside the transaction, `P` = `PRAGMA page_count` and the page
size `S`, and the main file's length `L` through a handle:

> **pending main-file growth** `Λ` = max(0, `P` × `S` − `L`)

- **Periodic check** (at `BEGIN` and after every 4,096 inserted rows of any table): continue iff `F_L` ≥ `M_L` + `Λ_k` + `I_L`,
  with `I_L` = 2 × max(16 MiB, `D_k`) and `D_k` the drop in free space since the previous check.
- **Final check** (immediately before `COMMIT`, after the verification and the final statements; nothing runs between):
  continue iff `F_L` ≥ `M_L` + `Λ_f` + `R_C`, with `R_C` = 1 MiB (defined; Q-24).
- **Equality continues.** Any unreadable input (the free-space query, `page_count`, the page size, the main length) rolls back as
  `LibraryFull`, like a value below the requirement. P0 and P5 keep `F_L` ≥ `M_L` + `G_p` + `J_p`, with `G_p` now counting the name
  bytes twice.
- **No journal term at the final check:** every page `COMMIT` writes was journalled when first changed, so `COMMIT` appends no
  record; **no rollback or recovery term:** a rollback restores pages in place and truncates.
- **What it promises:** StorageInventory's own model. If each interval's growth stays within `Λ_k` + `I_L`, and the growth after
  the final check within `Λ_f` + `R_C`, the import alone never takes the volume below `M_L`. **What it does not:** another program
  can consume space between a check and the write it covers; that residual race ends in a guard stop at the next check or in
  `SQLITE_FULL`, both rolling back without damage.

## 8. PERF-15 (a): key-range confinement (C4DR-M02)

### 8.1 The rule

In each source-prefixed dictionary index (`name (source_id, utf16)`, `folder_path_child`, `folder_path_root`), at every level:
**IN** pages are those whose key interval (from the dividers above them) intersects the target source's keys (for a new source,
whose keys sort past every key, the rightmost page of the level); every journalled page lies within **W = 2 positions** of the IN
run (else **REMOTE**, a defect); at most **2 × (W + 1) = 6** journalled pages of a level hold an entry of another source
(**SHARED**). Append-shaped trees: the rightmost page of each level and at most W pages to its left. Catalogue trees, page 1 and
free-list pages are allowed; any other page is a defect.

### 8.2 Status of W: what is proven and what is observed

- **From source (SQLite 3.53.3, `src/btree.c`, lines 8206 to 8325, read at the `version-3.53.3` tag, SHA-256
  `c0982890…7a94`):** `balance_nonroot` "redistributes cells on the iParentIdx'th child of pParent … and up to 2 siblings"
  (`NN 1`, `NB 3`), chosen among the page's immediate neighbours under the same parent (both from one side for the first or last
  child). **One balance step therefore rewrites pages at most two positions from the page that triggered it.**
- **Not proven:** a page rewritten by one step can receive the target's keys and trigger a later step one or two positions further;
  a chain of steps at a range's border could in principle exceed W.
- **Observed:** no analysed journal exceeds W: the largest distance is 2 (a new source's right edge: the rightmost page and its two
  left siblings per level) and 1 beside an existing source's range. A REMOTE page is reported and makes the outcome MISSED; it is
  never waived, and W changes only by reviewed change with the journal evidence.

## 9. Cancellation semantics (C4DR-M03)

- **Timer:** from the save token's cancellation to the Save lease's clean end (rolled back, T-OUTCOME recorded, writer, gate and
  spool released, interlock Idle).
- **Phases:** (1) detection, at most **0.5 s** anywhere (CAN-01d: token at every IMP-11 check, i.e. every 4,096 rows, and inside the
  verification after at most 65,536 examined rows); (2) rollback, 13 to 34 ms per MiB measured (0.378 s for the representative
  re-scan's 28.9 MiB here); (3) release (T-OUTCOME, closes, the lease's end).
- **Budgets:** total (PERF-16) **2 s** representative, **5 s** stress and worst-case; engine part = phases 1 and 2 (PERF-15 (c))
  **1.75 s** and **4.75 s**; recovery after a kill 5 s in every cell; stop thresholds 1.5×.
- **Why the verification needs checks:** it took 6.5 to 10.6 s at 2M in the reruns (and 12.2 s in the design review); without a
  check inside, a Stop pressed there waits for all of it.
- **Cancel points measured** (the worst one counts): after a token check during row insertion; at the start and the middle of the
  verification; immediately before `COMMIT`. C4's implementation repair tests all of them (C4-M02, D-R7); the harness's cancel at the
  end of row insertion measures phase 2 alone.
- **UI:** "Stopping…" at once; New scan unavailable until the lease ends; "undoing the partial save" after 2 s; Close waits;
  "Saved to History (it had finished before Stop)" when `COMMIT` came first.
- **Consistency:** CAN-01d, CAN-03, OBS-11, IMP-05, UI-10, PERF-15 (c), PERF-16, §15.4, TEST-P1, TEST-P2, §19 C4 and C5, D-53 all
  state the same numbers (checked by `spec_check.py`, group M03).

## 10. PERF-14 and PERF-16 decision (C4DR-M04)

**Chosen: Option A (an explicit engine share), with worst-case cells bounded by stop thresholds only.**

| Concept | Number | Applies to | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C5 end-to-end saving target** (P5 to P7) | **≤ 60 s** at 2M | representative and stress cells (spools from the generator) and the real drive; worst-case for information | C5 (TEST-P2) |
| **C4 engine target** (T-IMPORT alone) | **≤ 45 s** at 2M | representative and stress cells | C4 (TEST-P1) |
| **C4 architecture stop threshold** | **67.5 s** (1.5 × 45 s) representative and stress; **90 s** (1.5 × 60 s) worst-case | every 2M cell | C4 (STOP → design review) |

**Rationale.** The product requirement is PERF-14's: the user waits at most 60 s for "Saved to History" at 2M on the reference
machine, for every source the product is accepted for, which now means the representative and stress classes. The engine must
therefore leave room for C5's part: pass V and the error pass (two sequential reads of a 160 to 240 MB spool) and the spool's
decoding inside T-IMPORT, projected at 3 to 8 s (inferred; not measured until C5). A quarter of the budget, 15 s, is reserved for it,
a factor of about two over the projection for what is not yet measured (anti-malware scanning the spool, a cold spool cache). The
45 s are **not** fitted to the current runs: they are tighter than the stress first save measured under load (43.9 s) and than the
design review's worst-case cells (45 to 53 s), which are no longer bound by a target. The stop thresholds keep 1.5×, the factor the
prepared constant-factor alternative (a larger cache) was measured to recover. Option B (C4 informational only) was rejected
because it would let C4 pass an engine that C5 then cannot fit in 60 s, discovered only after C5 has started. If C5 measures more
than 15 s for its own part, the split is revised by reviewed change before C5 is accepted.

**PERF-16:** the total Stop Saving budgets of §9 (2 s / 5 s), C5's share 0.25 s (projected: one one-row transaction and three
closes), measured end to end in C5 with the split reported.
